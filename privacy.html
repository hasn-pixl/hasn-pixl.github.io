Privacy policy

HyperShorts · Last updated 9 November 2026

HyperShorts is a private tool used by the owner of the Zappardy YouTube channel to make videos and upload them to that channel. This page explains what data it uses and how.

What HyperShorts accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, HyperShorts asks for these permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (YouTube upload permission): to upload the videos HyperShorts makes and set the time each one goes public.
  • View your YouTube account (YouTube read-only permission): to read the channel's name and its list of scheduled videos, so a new video is scheduled after the last one.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload and schedule the channel owner's own videos.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. HyperShorts has no servers and does not send this data anywhere else.
  • HyperShorts does not sell, rent or share any data, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking.
  • HyperShorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

HyperShorts uses YouTube API Services. By using HyperShorts you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove HyperShorts' access at any time from your Google account's security settings (third-party access). Deleting the file youtube-token.json from the HyperShorts folder deletes the stored sign-in from the computer.

Children

HyperShorts is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
